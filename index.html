Why It Matters

Wildlife conservation is crucial for maintaining biodiversity and ecological balance. By reporting sightings, you provide valuable data that helps researchers and authorities monitor animal populations, track endangered species, and combat illegal poaching. Every report, big or small, contributes to a larger understanding of our natural world and how we can best protect it.
